Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: we never sell your data. Our assistant, Danisa, uses Google's Gemini AI to understand and answer you. Your chat history stays in your own browser. If you ask to talk to our founder, he gets your request, and Danisa takes notes of the call only if you tick the box (or say yes) to allow it. Request details and notes are deleted from our server after 24 hours.

1. Who We Are

REHABION is a rehabilitation technology startup based in Islamabad / Rawalpindi, Pakistan. We build game-based rehabilitation systems, AI physiotherapy tools, and biomedical engineering solutions. Our website is rehabion.github.io.

For privacy matters, contact us at: muhammadrmzn84@gmail.com

2. What We Collect, and When

Contact form. Your name, email, role and message, which are sent to our email inbox.

Talking to Danisa by text. What you type is sent to our server and on to Google's Gemini AI to produce a reply. We don't keep a copy of the conversation on our server. Your name and recent messages are saved in your own browser (local storage) so Danisa can carry on where you left off. Tap “Forget me” in the chat to erase them.

Talking to Danisa by voice. Your voice goes straight from your browser to Google's Gemini Live service, which hears you and answers. A written version of the conversation is kept only in your browser, as above.

“Listen” buttons. The reply text is sent to Google to be turned into speech.

Asking for the founder. We keep your name, what it is about, any contact you give, and a short summary of your chat with Danisa. These go to the founder as a phone notification, a Telegram message and an email.

A call with the founder. The call runs directly between your browser and his. Nobody's phone number is used or shown. Only if you agree, Danisa writes down what is said during the call. She uses Google's speech-to-text for this. Afterwards she writes a short report for the founder, sent to his Telegram. If you don't agree, nothing from the call is written down.

Security and limits. Like most websites, our server sees your IP address. We use it to stop spam and abuse, in counters that reset every hour. When you open Danisa, an invisible Cloudflare Turnstile check confirms you are a person and not a bot. We also keep simple daily totals (for example “34 chats today”) that identify no one.

Please don't share medical records or sensitive health details with Danisa. She gives general information, not medical advice.

3. How We Use It

  • To answer your questions and keep the conversation going
  • To connect you with our founder, and so he can follow up on what you discussed
  • To respond to your enquiry, collaboration or project proposal
  • To keep the service working and protected from abuse

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we never sell it.

4. Services That Process Data for Us

  • Google Gemini API (chat, voice, speech-to-text, text-to-speech). Google processes what you say or type to produce answers. Its terms for the Gemini API apply, and on our current plan Google may use the content to improve its products. So please don't share anything confidential with Danisa.
  • Cloudflare (runs our server and the Turnstile bot check). It stores request details and call notes for up to 24 hours.
  • Telegram (delivers alerts and call reports to the founder's own account).
  • Formspree (forwards the contact form, and copies of founder requests, to our email).
  • GitHub Pages (hosts this website; standard server logs).
  • Google Fonts (loads the typefaces; Google may log font requests).
  • Calendly and Gumroad (only if you choose to book a demo or buy a resource).

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. Danisa's memory is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device unless you send a message. Cloudflare Turnstile may use a short-lived cookie or similar signal to tell people from bots.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Founder requests, call notes and our copy of the report: deleted automatically from our server after 24 hours
  • Abuse counters: reset every hour or day
  • Reports and alerts already delivered to the founder (Telegram, email): kept as long as needed to follow up with you, and deleted on request
  • Contact-form emails: kept as long as needed for your enquiry, and deleted on request
  • Your browser's copy of chats: until you tap “Forget me” or clear your browser data

7. Your Rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can also refuse note-taking on a call, and change your mind at any time. Email muhammadrmzn84@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Data Security

All traffic uses HTTPS or encrypted WebRTC. Founder links are random and single-purpose, and expire after a day. Our server's keys are kept in Cloudflare's secret storage, never in the website's code. No internet service is 100% secure, but we work to keep the data we hold small and short-lived.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

When we change how the website handles data, we update this page and the date at the top.
